Click-casting checklist

EUI Click Trace is our temporary logger, not an official EllesmereUI addon.

Enable once

  1. Fully restart WoW and enable EUI Click Trace in AddOns.
  2. /euict status should show ON and zero errors/unavailable events. If OFF: /euict on.
  3. Cast a heal and check status again: the event count should increase.

Mark a failure

/euict mark tank stuck

Type this when safe, or put it in a macro on a spare key. It captures up to 60 seconds before and 20 seconds after. Avoid repeat marks within 20 seconds.

Recovery tests

Keep the group alive. Stop at the first success. Use right-click Flash Heal, stand still, stay in range, and wait for casts/global cooldowns.

  1. Click an uncovered spot on the tank frame.
  2. Move into empty space, return, and retry.
  3. Hover another player without casting, return, and retry.
  4. Target the tank and cast normal Flash Heal from the action bar, cursor away from frames. Then retry click-casting.
  5. Click-cast on another player, return to the tank, and retry.

Note the successful step, any red error or targeting cursor, and whether the cast bar starts. Note if marking itself fixes it.

Optional settings tests between pulls

Change one at a time, out of combat. Restore it before the next test.

  1. Buff Tooltips: Hidden, via the cog beside Show Raid Frames Tooltip. Keep defensive icons visible.
  2. Hide debuff tooltips, including the Dispellable tile override.
  3. Trigger Bindings on Down: off to cast on release.
Use Hidden, not Hidden In Combat. Record failures and pulls tested. Restore your original settings afterward.

Save and report

After testing and the 20-second window, /reload or log out normally to save the log.
